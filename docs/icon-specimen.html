Bedouin · identity

bayt al‑sha’ar

The black goat‑hair tent: the house you strike, carry, and pitch again identically on new ground. One mark, two levels of detail, an entrance cut clean through, built to survive a 16 pixel favicon.

Hero mark

Low, wide, and slack

Two poles, one sagging bay, guy lines staked outward. Shown on both grounds so neither theme is an afterthought.

Construction

Two decisions the drawing turns on

Every slope is concave

Fabric hangs, so each slope is steep at the pole and flattens toward the eave. A mountain silhouette is convex or straight — the concavity is the one thing that stops the mark reading as terrain. Earlier passes with near‑straight flanks rendered as a plain pyramid at every size.

The ridge sags

A generic tent icon is an isosceles triangle. Slack cloth dipping between poles is what this tent actually looks like, and the dip is the feature that still reads at 16 pixels.

The accent is the rope

One colour, and it lands on the guy lines rather than as decoration — the ropes are what let you strike the tent and pitch it again somewhere else, which is what the tool does. The red is sadu madder, from the weaving, not desert terracotta.

The 16px question

It holds — but not as monoline

The handoff found the ridge tent muddies below roughly 24 pixels. It does, as a monoline: a hairline on a hanging curve is exactly what fills in. The reduced mark is solid instead, and keeps the sag.

Actually rasterised, then magnified

Not a scaled illustration — these are the real renders at 16, 24, and 32 pixels, blown up so you can count the pixels.

At 16 pixels the two peaks and the dip survive; the madder ground line thins to a single row. Below 16 the sag closes and the mark should not be used — ship a 16 px raster rather than letting a browser scale further down.

The entrance

A hole, not a second colour

The doorway is cut with fill-rule="evenodd", so the ground shows through and one path serves both themes. It is a trapezoid rather than an arch: that is the tent‑door convention, and flaring downward echoes the tent’s own silhouette, so the opening reads as a small tent.

The entrance resolves cleanly down to 24 pixels. At 16 it survives as a two‑pixel notch, which is legible but soft — use bedouin-mark-tiny.svg there instead.
